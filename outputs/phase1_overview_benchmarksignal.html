Competitive Moat

Layer 1: Extraction Engine

Vision-LLM extraction from heterogeneous international PDFs. Custom prompts tuned across 15+ report formats, 4 languages.

Time to replicate: 6–12 months

Layer 2: KPI Taxonomy

Canonical mapping of company-specific KPIs (Recurring EBIT, RCO, RCOBD, Adjusted EBITDA). Domain knowledge as data.

Time to replicate: 3–6 months per vertical

Layer 3: Corporate-First UX

Every screen designed for CFO/CSO/IRO workflows. Earnings prep, board packages, ESG trajectories. No investor paradigm.

Incumbents need: separate product line

Layer 4: Price Point

CHF 48–72K/yr vs $100K+ for Bloomberg/FactSet bundles. No trading data, no portfolio analytics = leaner cost.

Incumbents: can't unbundle

Data Flywheel

Every report processed improves extraction accuracy. Every company onboarded enriches the KPI taxonomy. This compounds over time and creates switching costs.

Verdict

Recommendation

CONDITIONAL GO

Three conditions before full commitment:

1. Validate extraction accuracy on 5 real reports (Holcim, Heidelberg, CRH, Vicat, Buzzi) in Week 0–1. Target: 95%+ on core financial KPIs.

2. Secure verbal pilot commitment from Holcim (or equivalent) before Week 3.

3. Cap build investment at USD 5,000 in infrastructure/API costs for first 8 weeks.

Recommended First Milestone: Extraction PoC (Week 0–1)

Deliverable: Extract financial KPIs from 5 historical annual reports, normalize to CHF, map to canonical taxonomy, produce a single comparison table.

Success criteria:

  • 95%+ accuracy on core financial KPIs
  • Cross-currency normalization working
  • KPI taxonomy correctly maps company-specific definitions
  • Total extraction time under 10 minutes per report

This validates the core technical risk before committing to the full 8–10 week build.

## 2. Demand Signals & Customer Pain

### 2.1 What Corporate Teams Currently Struggle With

**Strategy teams:**
- "Time-consuming manual research -- aggregating data from disparate sources drains analyst hours" [DFIN]
- "Finding truly comparable companies is difficult, as even businesses in the same sector may differ in size, customer base, geographic reach, or growth stage" [Corporate Finance Institute]
- Peer benchmarking is described as "a vexing task for financial analysts but one that is critical for forecasting earnings" [CFA Institute]

**IR teams:**
- 64% of IR professionals cited "lack of customization" as the primary reason for switching from generic CRMs to dedicated IR software [IR Magazine, 2025]
- IR teams must prepare peer comparisons before every earnings call, board meeting, and investor day -- typically under severe time pressure

**Controllers / FP&A:**
- Manually pulling data from 10--20 peer annual reports for quarterly board packages
- Normalizing different reporting standards (IFRS vs US GAAP), currencies, and fiscal year-ends
- ESG metrics are especially inconsistent across peers -- no two companies report them the same way

### 2.2 How Long Manual Peer Analysis Takes Today

**Available data points:**

- AI has the potential to "turn weeks of manual peer review into minutes" [DFIN]
- Technology can "reduce time spent on manual research by 99%" [Hanover Research]
- AlphaSense users leverage Smart Summaries to "extract the most crucial insights from each [earnings] call in just minutes" -- implying previous process took hours [AlphaSense]
- Valona Intelligence promises to "track more companies more consistently" -- implying manual tracking is inconsistent and time-consuming [Valona]

**Estimated time for manual peer benchmarking (based on industry consensus):**

| Task | Manual Time | Automated Time |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Collecting annual/quarterly reports for 15 peers | 2--4 hours | 0 (auto-ingested) |
| Extracting financial KPIs from each report | 8--16 hours | Minutes |
| Normalizing currencies, fiscal years, accounting standards | 4--8 hours | Minutes |
| Building comparison tables and charts | 4--8 hours | Instant |
| Writing narrative analysis / commentary | 4--8 hours | Minutes (AI-generated) |
| **Total per benchmarking cycle** | **22--44 hours (3--5 working days)** | **< 1 hour** |

**Note:** No single public survey provides an exact "X hours per cycle" figure. The 3--5 day estimate is derived from the "weeks of manual peer review" language used by DFIN and the 99% time reduction claim from Hanover Research.

### 2.3 Tools Currently Used

| Tool | Typical Annual Cost / Seat | What It Does | Limitations for Peer Benchmarking |
|------|---------------------------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Bloomberg Terminal | USD 28,320--31,980 | Real-time market data, news, comps | Expensive; data terminals, not automated analysis; manual peer group setup |
| FactSet | USD 12,000--18,000 | Financial data, screening, modeling | Requires manual query building; limited ESG data extraction from reports |
| S&P Capital IQ | USD 12,000--30,000 | Financial data, screening, comps | Structured data only -- does not extract KPIs from PDF reports |
| Refinitiv Eikon (LSEG) | USD 14,000--22,000 | Market data, screening | Similar limitations to Bloomberg |
| AlphaSense | USD 10,000--20,000 | Document search, earnings transcripts | Search/retrieval tool, not a structured benchmarking platform |
| Manual Excel | Free (labor cost) | Custom analysis | Extremely time-consuming, error-prone, not scalable |

**Key gap:** None of these tools automatically extract KPIs from newly published reports and deliver normalized peer comparisons. They provide raw data or search capabilities -- the synthesis is still manual.

### 2.4 Exact Customer Language for Pain

**From CFA Institute (2024):**
> "Peer benchmarking is a vexing task for financial analysts but one that is critical for forecasting earnings."
> "Analysts spend a large portion of their time carefully selecting peers because this step directly affects the accuracy and usefulness of the results."

**From DFIN (Donnelley Financial Solutions):**

**From Glassdoor / job postings (Strategy Analyst roles):**
- Job descriptions consistently require "specific experience in market sizing or peer benchmarking"
- Strategy Analyst salary range: USD 80,000--105,000/year -- these are the people spending days on manual peer analysis

**From Valona Intelligence marketing:**
> "[Valona] automatically reviews releases, transcripts, and slides to deliver analyst-level earnings summaries for every competitor, every quarter."
-- The positioning itself reveals the pain: teams cannot currently track every competitor consistently.

### 2.5 Key Purchase Triggers

1. **Earnings season (4x per year):** Strategy and IR teams scramble to benchmark peer results within hours of publication. The quarterly crunch is the single strongest trigger.
2. **Board meetings / strategy reviews:** Board packages require up-to-date peer comparisons. CFOs and CSOs need current data, not 3-month-old analysis.
3. **M&A due diligence:** Acquirers benchmark targets against peer sets for valuation multiples and operational KPIs.
4. **Regulatory reporting deadlines:** CSRD, ISSB, and other ESG mandates require peer-referenced disclosures.
5. **New competitor activity:** IPOs, major acquisitions, or strategic pivots by peers trigger ad-hoc benchmarking needs.
6. **CEO/CFO onboarding:** New executives need a comprehensive competitive landscape within weeks of starting.

### 3.2 Shift from Manual Analyst Work to Automated Extraction

- Vision-capable LLMs now "understand document structure, extract meaning from context, and adapt to new formats without reprogramming" [Virtido, 2026]
- Stanford CS231N research (2025) demonstrates computer vision applied to financial statement analysis -- directly relevant to BenchmarkSignal's approach
- Cognica (November 2025) published a technical guide on "Automated Financial Statement Extraction from PDFs Using LLMs"
- The "Talk to EDGAR" platform allows plain-English queries against SEC filings -- demonstrating that structured extraction from financial documents is production-ready
- LlamaIndex offers an "AI OCR Processing Platform" specifically for automated financial data extraction

**Key insight:** The technology for automated KPI extraction from financial reports has matured significantly in 2025--2026. What was research-grade 2 years ago is now production-deployable.

## 5. Regulatory & Compliance Context

### 5.1 CSRD (EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)

**Timeline:**

| Wave | Reporting Year | Report Published | Companies Affected |
|------|---------------|------------------|--------------------|
| Wave 1 | FY 2024 | 2025 | Large listed EU companies already subject to NFRD (~11,700) |
| Wave 2 | FY 2025 | 2026 | All non-listed EU large companies (originally ~39,000) |
| Wave 3 | FY 2026 | 2027 | Listed SMEs |

**Omnibus Package (December 2025) -- Major Scope Reduction:**
- New thresholds raised to >1,000 FTE employees AND EUR 450M+ net turnover (was 250+ employees)
- Wave 2 and Wave 3 companies received a "stop-the-clock" postponement
- EFRAG's revised ESRS draft shows 61% reduction in mandatory datapoints (from ~1,100 to ~430)
- Voluntary disclosures eliminated entirely

**Impact on BenchmarkSignal:**
- Even with reduced scope, thousands of EU companies must now report ESG metrics in standardized formats
- These reports are the exact input that BenchmarkSignal extracts and benchmarks
- Companies NEED peer ESG benchmarks to know if their metrics are competitive
- The standardization actually HELPS automated extraction (more consistent formats)

### 5.2 ISSB Standards (IFRS S1/S2)

**Global adoption status (January 2026):**
- **21 jurisdictions** have adopted ISSB standards (voluntary or mandatory) with reporting starting 2024--2026 [S&P Global]
- **30+ jurisdictions** are moving toward mandatory reporting [KPMG]

**Key jurisdiction timelines:**

| Jurisdiction | Status | Mandatory From |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| UK | Endorsement underway | Expected 2026--2027 |
| Australia | Mandatory for large entities | 2025 (phased) |
| Canada | Mandatory for large reporting issuers | 2025 (phased) |
| Japan | Under consideration | TBD |
| Malaysia | Large listed issuers | 2025--2026 (phased) |
| Pakistan | Mandatory for large listed | July 2025 |
| Qatar | Adopted | January 2026 |
| Taiwan | Roadmap published | 2026 |
| California (US state) | Mandatory climate reporting | January 2026 |

**Impact on BenchmarkSignal:**
- ISSB creates a global baseline for sustainability disclosures -- more data to extract and benchmark
- Accelerated adoption in 2026--2027 means a growing universe of comparable ESG data
- Companies adopting ISSB will need to benchmark against early adopters

### 5.3 SEC Climate Disclosure Rules

**Current status (as of May 2026):**
- SEC adopted climate disclosure rules on March 6, 2024
- SEC issued a voluntary stay in April 2024 pending litigation
- On March 27, 2025, SEC voted to **end its defense** of the rules
- The rules are now defended by a coalition of 19 state attorneys general
- **Effectively dead at the federal level under the current Commission**

**However:**
- California's climate disclosure laws (SB 253, SB 261) proceed independently, requiring reporting from January 2026
- Investor pressure continues to drive voluntary climate disclosure
- Companies with EU operations still subject to CSRD regardless

### 5.4 How Regulations CREATE Demand for Automated Benchmarking

1. **Volume explosion:** Thousands of companies publishing standardized sustainability reports for the first time creates a massive new corpus of comparable data
2. **Peer context is required:** CSRD's double materiality assessment requires understanding peer performance. Companies cannot report in isolation.
3. **Board-level urgency:** Sustainability is now a board-agenda item. Directors need peer benchmarks to assess management performance on ESG.
4. **Audit requirements:** CSRD mandates limited assurance (moving to reasonable assurance) -- companies need to benchmark their data quality against peers
5. **Competitive pressure:** Early CSRD reporters set the standard. Late adopters need to benchmark against leaders to calibrate their own disclosures.
